📝 Lesson 9 Official Assessment

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 9: Sentence Types and Functions.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the type of sentence being described.

1. A sentence that makes a statement.

2. A sentence that asks a question.

3. A sentence that gives a command or makes a request.

4. A sentence that expresses strong emotion.

5. The sentence type used to provide information.

6. The sentence type that usually ends with a question mark.

7. The sentence type that can end with a period or an exclamation point depending on its force.

8. The sentence type that commonly ends with an exclamation point when expressing strong emotion.

9. Identify the type: "Please close the door."

10. Identify the type: "What a wonderful performance!"

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Choose the best answer.

1. What type of sentence is "The library closes at six o'clock"?

2. What type of sentence is "Did you finish your homework?"?

3. What type of sentence is "Please submit your project tomorrow"?

4. What type of sentence is "That was an incredible performance!"?

5. Which sentence is used to ask for information?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Explain the four types of sentences according to their functions. Write one original example for each type.
