📝 Lesson 10 Official Assessment

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 10: Reading and Understanding Short Texts.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the term being described.

1. The central message or most important point of a text.

2. Information that explains or supports the main idea.

3. A short written passage that communicates information or tells a story.

4. The person, place, thing, or event being discussed in a text.

5. The meaning or message that the author wants readers to understand.

6. The process of understanding and interpreting written information.

7. Information directly stated in a text.

8. A conclusion reached by using clues and information from a text.

9. A brief explanation of the most important points of a text.

10. The person who writes a text.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Read each question carefully based on the passage below.

"Lara enjoys reading books during her free time. She visits the school library every Friday to borrow stories and informational books. Reading helps her learn new words, understand different ideas, and improve her communication skills. Because of her interest in reading, Lara encourages her classmates to visit the library too."

1. What is the main idea of the passage?

2. When does Lara visit the school library?

3. Which detail supports the idea that reading helps Lara learn?

4. What can be inferred about Lara?

5. What is the author's main purpose in the passage?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

"Many students use the internet to help with their schoolwork. It allows them to search for information, watch educational videos, and access digital learning materials. However, students should check whether the information they find is reliable and accurate."

Read the passage above and explain its main idea and two supporting details.
