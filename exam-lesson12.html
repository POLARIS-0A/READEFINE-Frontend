📝 Quiz 12: Identifying Main Ideas

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 12.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the term being described.

1. The central point or most important message of a paragraph.

2. The general subject being discussed in a text.

3. Information that explains or provides evidence for the central point.

4. A main idea that is directly expressed in a paragraph.

5. A main idea that readers must determine from the supporting details.

6. The process of identifying the most important information in a paragraph.

7. The information that explains, describes, or gives examples of the central point.

8. The general subject that a paragraph focuses on.

9. A sentence that directly communicates the central message of a paragraph.

10. The overall message that the author wants readers to understand.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Choose the letter of the best answer.

1. What is the main idea of a paragraph?

2. Read the paragraph: "Dogs make wonderful pets. They can provide companionship, help protect homes, and bring happiness to families." What is the main idea?

3. What do supporting details do?

4. Which statement describes an implied main idea?

5. What should a reader do first when identifying the main idea?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why is identifying the main idea important when reading? Explain how the main idea and supporting details work together to help readers understand a paragraph.
