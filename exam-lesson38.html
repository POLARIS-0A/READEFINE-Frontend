📝 Quiz 38: Research and Information Literacy

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 38.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. A systematic process of gathering and analyzing information about a topic.

2. The ability to recognize, locate, evaluate, and use information responsibly.

3. Questions that guide an investigation.

4. The process of checking whether information sources are reliable.

5. Presenting another person's words or ideas as one's own without proper acknowledgment.

6. The acknowledgment of sources used in a written work.

7. A citation style developed by the American Psychological Association.

8. The process of arranging and examining collected information.

9. The act of giving proper credit to the original author.

10. The responsible use of information without misleading others or violating academic honesty.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. What is the first important step in conducting research?

2. Which is an example of plagiarism?

3. Why is citation important?

4. Which is a common citation style?

5. What should a student do before using information from a website?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why are research and information literacy important for students? Explain how students can avoid plagiarism and use information responsibly.
