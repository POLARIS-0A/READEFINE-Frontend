Level 2 • Lesson 17

Paragraph Structure and Organization

← Back to Lessons
🎯 Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • Define a paragraph.
  • Identify the three main parts of a paragraph.
  • Explain the purpose of a topic sentence, supporting sentences, and concluding sentence.
  • Organize ideas into a clear and meaningful paragraph.
💡 Introduction & Discussion

Writing is made up of sentences that communicate ideas. These sentences are grouped into paragraphs to organize information and make writing easier to understand.
A paragraph is a group of related sentences that develop one main idea.
Understanding paragraph structure helps learners arrange their thoughts, connect sentences, and communicate information clearly.

1. What Is a Paragraph?

A paragraph is a group of related sentences that discuss one main idea or topic. It usually begins with a topic sentence, continues with supporting sentences, and may end with a concluding sentence.

Example: Reading is a useful habit for students. It helps improve vocabulary and reading comprehension. It also provides new information and develops imagination. Therefore, students should make time for reading every day.

The sentences work together to explain one main idea: reading is a useful habit for students.

2. Topic Sentence

The topic sentence introduces the main idea of a paragraph. It tells readers what the paragraph will discuss. It is commonly found at the beginning of a paragraph.

Example: "Regular exercise is important for maintaining good health."

3. Supporting Sentences

Supporting sentences provide explanations, examples, facts, and other information that develop the topic sentence.

Topic Sentence: Regular exercise is important for maintaining good health.
Supporting Sentences:
• Exercise strengthens the muscles.
• It helps improve endurance.
• It supports overall physical wellness.

4. Concluding Sentence

A concluding sentence ends a paragraph by restating the main idea or providing a final thought. It gives the paragraph a sense of completion.

Example: "Because of these benefits, regular exercise should be part of a healthy lifestyle."

5. Unity in a Paragraph

Unity means that all sentences in a paragraph are related to one main idea. Every sentence must contribute to the topic.

Topic Sentence: Online learning provides flexibility for students.
✔ Students can access lessons from different locations. (Related)
✔ Recorded lessons can be reviewed when needed. (Related)
✖ My favorite food is fried chicken. (Unrelated detail!)

6. Coherence in a Paragraph

Coherence means that ideas and sentences are connected in a logical and understandable way. Writers use transition words to connect sentences smoothly.

Transition Words: First, Next, Also, Furthermore, However, Therefore, Finally.

Example: First, students should identify their tasks. Next, they should organize them according to importance. Finally, they should complete each task based on their schedule.

7. How to Organize a Paragraph

  • Choose one clear topic.
  • Identify the main idea.
  • Write a topic sentence.
  • Add supporting sentences.
  • Arrange ideas in a logical order.
  • Use transition words when needed.
  • End with a concluding sentence when appropriate.
📌 Summary Examples

Example 1: Paragraph Parts

"Time management is important for students..."

Contains: Topic Sentence, Supporting Sentences, and Concluding Sentence.

Example 2: Identifying Unity

Healthy eating topic ➡️ Fruits provide vitamins, veggies have nutrients, balanced diet supports functions.

All sentences support healthy eating.

Example 3: Identifying Coherence

"First, prepare your notebook. Next, write the important information. Finally, review your notes."

Transitions connect the steps smoothly.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • A paragraph is a group of related sentences about one main idea.
  • The topic sentence introduces the main idea.
  • Supporting sentences explain and develop the topic sentence.
  • The concluding sentence gives the paragraph a final thought.
  • Unity means keeping all sentences focused on the main topic.
  • Coherence means ideas are connected in a logical and understandable way.
  • Transition words help connect sentences and ideas smoothly.
