📝 Quiz 40: Practical Literacy in Everyday Situations

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 40.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. The ability to apply reading, writing, and communication skills to everyday situations.

2. Reading information for a specific practical purpose.

3. Writing information needed to complete everyday tasks.

4. The ability to use digital technologies and information responsibly.

5. The ability to understand financial information and make informed money-related decisions.

6. Understanding information related to personal health and safety.

7. Reading information such as instructions, signs, and announcements to complete daily tasks.

8. A written document that lists purchased items and their corresponding prices.

9. Information that explains the qualifications and responsibilities of a job.

10. The ability to understand and use information when participating in daily responsibilities.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. Which is an example of practical literacy?

2. Which situation demonstrates digital literacy?

3. Which situation demonstrates financial literacy?

4. Why is it important to understand safety instructions?

5. Which is an example of functional writing?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Explain why practical literacy is important in everyday life. Give at least three examples of situations where you can use literacy skills to make responsible decisions.
