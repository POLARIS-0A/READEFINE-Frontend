📝 Quiz 16: Understanding Text Structure

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 16.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the type of text structure or term being described.

1. The way information and ideas are organized in a written text.

2. A structure that explains the characteristics or qualities of a subject.

3. A structure that presents events or steps in the order they happen.

4. A structure that identifies similarities between two or more subjects.

5. A structure that identifies differences between two or more subjects.

6. A structure that explains why something happens and what results from it.

7. The reason something happens.

8. The result or outcome of an event.

9. A structure that presents an issue and explains possible ways to address it.

10. Words or phrases that help readers recognize how information is organized.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Choose the letter of the best answer.

1. What does text structure refer to?

2. Which text structure explains similarities and differences?

3. Read the sentence: "The roads became slippery because it rained heavily." Which text structure is used?

4. Which signal word commonly indicates a sequence?

5. A passage explains that students have difficulty organizing their assignments and suggests using a planner. What structure is used?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why is understanding text structure important when reading? Explain at least two types of text structure and provide an example of each.
