Level 1 • Lesson 5

Parts of Speech

← Back to Lessons
🎯 Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • 1. Identify the major parts of speech.
  • 2. Explain the function of each part of speech.
  • 3. Recognize parts of speech in sentences.
  • 4. Construct sentences using different parts of speech.
💡 Introduction & Discussion

Every word in a sentence has a role. Some words name people, places, and things. Others describe actions, qualities, or relationships.
These roles are called parts of speech.

1. Noun

A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, or idea.

  • Person: teacher, student
  • Place: school, library
  • Thing: computer, notebook
  • Idea: freedom, honesty
Sentence: The student opened the computer.

2. Pronoun

A pronoun is a word used in place of a noun (e.g., he, she, it, they, we, I, you).

Sentence: Maria is studying. She has an examination tomorrow.
The pronoun "she" replaces Maria.

3. Verb

A verb expresses an action, occurrence, or state of being (e.g., write, study, think, is, become).

Sentence: The students study every evening.

4. Adjective

An adjective describes or modifies a noun or pronoun (e.g., beautiful, intelligent, tall, difficult).

Sentence: She submitted a difficult assignment.
The word "difficult" describes assignment.

5. Adverb

An adverb modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb. It often explains how, when, where, or to what extent (e.g., quickly, carefully, yesterday, very).

Sentence: He answered the question carefully.
The word "carefully" describes how he answered.

6. Preposition

A preposition shows the relationship of a noun or pronoun to another word (e.g., in, on, at, under, beside, between).

Sentence: The notebook is on the table.

7. Conjunction

A conjunction connects words, phrases, or clauses (e.g., and, but, or, because, although).

Sentence: I wanted to study, but I felt tired.

8. Interjection

An interjection expresses a sudden emotion or reaction (e.g., Wow!, Ouch!, Oh!, Hurray!).

Sentence: Wow! Your presentation was excellent.

🔑 Key Takeaways

The eight major parts of speech are:

  • 1. Noun  •  2. Pronoun  •  3. Verb  •  4. Adjective
  • 5. Adverb  •  6. Preposition  •  7. Conjunction  •  8. Interjection
  • Each part of speech has a specific function in sentence construction.
