📝 Quiz 11: Reading Comprehension Strategies

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 11.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the reading comprehension strategy or term being described.

1. The ability to understand the meaning of written words, sentences, and paragraphs.

2. A strategy that involves looking at the title, headings, and pictures before reading a text completely.

3. Making an educated guess about what may happen or what information may appear in a text.

4. Reading a text quickly to understand its general idea.

5. Looking quickly through a text to find specific information such as names, dates, and numbers.

6. Creating mental pictures of the people, places, and events described in a text.

7. Relating a text to personal experiences, other texts, or real-world events.

8. Giving a shorter version of a text using only its most important ideas.

9. Checking whether the information being read is clear and understandable.

10. A strategy that involves using questions before, during, and after reading.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Choose the letter of the best answer.

1. What is the main purpose of reading comprehension?

2. A learner examines the title, headings, and pictures before reading an article. What strategy is being used?

3. A student looks through a school announcement to find the date of an event. Which strategy is used?

4. A learner imagines a beautiful garden while reading its description in a story. What strategy is being applied?

5. After reading a long passage, a learner writes only its most important ideas in a shorter form. What strategy is used?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why are reading comprehension strategies important when reading a text? Explain at least two strategies and how they can help you understand what you read.
