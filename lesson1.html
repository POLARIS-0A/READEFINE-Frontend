Level 1 • Lesson 1

Understanding Words and Vocabulary

← Back to Levels

🎯 Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • Explain what vocabulary means.
  • Identify the importance of vocabulary in communication.
  • Understand unfamiliar words through examples.
  • Use appropriate words in sentences.

📖 Introduction

Words are an important part of everyday communication. We use words when speaking, reading, writing, sending messages, and expressing our thoughts.

Understanding words helps people communicate clearly and understand information. Learning new words also allows individuals to express their ideas more accurately.

📝 Lesson Discussion

1. What Is Vocabulary?

Vocabulary refers to the collection of words that a person knows, understands, and uses in communication.

A person's vocabulary develops through reading, listening, speaking, writing, and learning new information.

Example: A student who knows the words "confident," "responsible," and "determined" can use these words to describe people, actions, and situations.

2. Types of Vocabulary

Receptive Vocabulary

Words that a person can understand when reading or listening but may not regularly use when speaking or writing.

Example: Understanding "generous" in a story.
Expressive Vocabulary

Words that a person can actively use when speaking or writing.

Example: Using "generous" to describe a helpful person.

3. Importance of Vocabulary

  • Improves reading comprehension.
  • Helps people express ideas clearly.
  • Supports effective communication.
  • Makes writing more meaningful.
  • Helps learners understand academic and everyday information.

4. How to Improve Vocabulary

  • Read books, articles, and other written materials.
  • Listen carefully to conversations and discussions.
  • Look up unfamiliar words in a dictionary.
  • Write down new words and their meanings.
  • Practice using new words in sentences.

💡 Vocabulary Examples

Word: Responsible
Meaning: Able to be trusted to do what is expected or required.
Sentence: A responsible student submits assignments on time.
Word: Hesitant
Meaning: Uncertain or unsure about doing something.
Sentence: She was hesitant to answer because she was not confident.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Vocabulary is a collection of words a person knows and uses.
  • Receptive vocabulary involves understanding words.
  • Expressive vocabulary involves actively using words.
  • A strong vocabulary improves communication and comprehension.
