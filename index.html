THE PROBLEM

Today, many young learners are not able to read or understand certain English information across different types of communication and media. According to the National Literacy Institute (2026), around 739 million adults worldwide still lack basic literacy skills in 2024. However, a majority of 93% of young people aged 15 to 24 do indeed have the ability to read and write, while 7% may struggle with literacy. As small of a percentage it may seem, it is still important to nurture English literacy skills for each individual, as it is the key for many social activities and the enhancement of critical thinking, which opens pathways for better futures and career pathways, as English is a universal language used by many. Here at READEFINE, we believe that Illiteracy is caused by lack the confidence and the proper support, which we aim to solve.

THE VISION

To create a website that eliminates illiteracy, and begin your metamorphosis towards literacy mastery.

THE MISSION

1. Provide lessons with clear study guides and manageable steps.
2. Help learners understand difficult topics through proper support and explanation: what they mean, and how to use them.
3. Enhanced interactivity and engagement through VOICED LESSONS.
4. Visualize their level of mastery in English fundamentals through PROGRESS BARS and TESTS.
