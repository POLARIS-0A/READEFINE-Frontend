📝 Quiz 26: Comparing and Contrasting Texts

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 26.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. The process of identifying similarities between two or more subjects.

2. The process of identifying differences between two or more subjects.

3. A signal word that indicates similarity.

4. A signal word that indicates contrast.

5. A method that compares subjects one feature at a time.

6. A method that discusses one subject completely before discussing another.

7. A word that indicates two things share similar qualities.

8. A phrase that introduces a difference between ideas.

9. The process of examining two or more texts to identify their similarities and differences.

10. Information that shows how two subjects are not alike.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. Which word commonly signals comparison?

2. Which phrase commonly signals contrast?

3. What is the purpose of comparing texts?

4. Which method discusses one feature at a time?

5. "Both dogs and cats can be kept as household pets." What does the sentence show?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Compare and contrast online learning and face-to-face learning. Give at least one similarity and two differences.
