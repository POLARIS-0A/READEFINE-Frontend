📝 Quiz 31: Advanced Vocabulary in Context

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 31.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. Words commonly used in academic, professional, or formal communication.

2. Understanding an unfamiliar word through surrounding information.

3. The literal or dictionary meaning of a word.

4. The feelings or associations connected to a word.

5. Words that have similar meanings.

6. Words that have opposite meanings.

7. Words that share the same spelling or pronunciation but have different meanings.

8. Words formed from the same root word.

9. A type of connotation that creates a favorable impression.

10. A type of connotation that creates an unfavorable impression.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. What does the word "commence" mean?

2. Which pair consists of synonyms?

3. What does denotation refer to?

4. Which word has a generally positive connotation?

5. Why are context clues useful?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why is developing advanced vocabulary important in academic and everyday communication? Give at least two examples of advanced vocabulary words and their meanings.
