📝 Quiz 17: Paragraph Structure and Organization

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 17.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the term being described.

1. A group of related sentences that develop one main idea.

2. The sentence that introduces the main idea of a paragraph.

3. Sentences that explain or develop the topic sentence.

4. A sentence that ends a paragraph by restating the main idea or giving a final thought.

5. The quality of a paragraph in which all sentences support one main idea.

6. The quality of a paragraph in which ideas are connected logically and clearly.

7. Words or phrases used to connect ideas and sentences.

8. The central point discussed in a paragraph.

9. The process of arranging ideas in a clear and meaningful order.

10. The sentence that tells readers what a paragraph will discuss.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Choose the letter of the best answer.

1. What is a paragraph?

2. Which part of a paragraph introduces its main idea?

3. What is the purpose of supporting sentences?

4. Which term refers to keeping all sentences related to one main idea?

5. Which word can be used to show the final step in an organized paragraph?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Explain the three main parts of a paragraph and describe how unity and coherence make a paragraph easier to understand.
