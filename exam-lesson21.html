📝 Quiz 21: Fact and Opinion

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 21.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. A statement that can be proven through evidence.

2. A statement that expresses a personal belief or judgment.

3. Information used to verify whether a statement is true or false.

4. A word that expresses a person's personal belief, such as "I think."

5. A statement that can be checked through reliable records.

6. A personal preference that may differ among individuals.

7. The process of determining whether information is factual or opinion-based.

8. A statement that expresses what someone believes should happen.

9. Information that supports the truth of a statement.

10. A personal judgment about a subject.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. Which statement is a fact?

2. Which statement expresses an opinion?

3. Which expression commonly introduces an opinion?

4. Why is it important to identify facts and opinions?

5. Which statement needs evidence to be verified?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why is it important to distinguish facts from opinions when reading information online or on social media? Give one example.
