📝 Quiz 33: Advanced Grammar and Sentence Correction

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 33.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. A grammar rule requiring subjects and verbs to agree in number and person.

2. The correct and consistent use of verb tenses.

3. A grammar rule requiring pronouns to correspond correctly to the nouns they refer to.

4. A word or group of words that describes another word.

5. An incomplete group of words presented as a sentence.

6. A sentence containing independent clauses joined incorrectly.

7. A verb form used to describe an action happening now or regularly.

8. A verb form used to describe an action completed in the past.

9. The process of identifying and correcting grammatical errors.

10. The process of improving a sentence by correcting its grammar and structure.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. Which sentence demonstrates correct subject-verb agreement?

2. Which sentence uses the correct past tense?

3. Which is a sentence fragment?

4. Which sentence correctly fixes the run-on sentence?
"I was hungry I prepared some food."

5. What is the purpose of sentence correction?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why is grammar important in academic and professional communication? Give at least two examples of grammatical errors and explain how they can be corrected.
