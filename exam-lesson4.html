📝 Lesson 4 Official Assessment

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 4: Homophones and Commonly Confused Words.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the correct term or word.

1. Words that sound alike but have different meanings and often different spellings.

2. The correct word to complete: "___ going to school today."

3. The correct word to complete: "The book is over ___."

4. The correct word to complete: "I can ___ the birds singing."

5. The correct word to complete: "Please ___ your name on the paper."

6. The correct word to complete: "The students left ___ bags in the classroom."

7. The correct word to complete: "The dog wagged ___ tail."

8. The correct word to complete: "___ is my favorite subject."

9. The correct word to complete: "I have ___ books than you."

10. The correct word to complete: "She is taller ___ her brother."

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Choose the best answer.

1. Which pair consists of homophones?

2. Which sentence uses their correctly?

3. Which word completes the sentence? "I ___ a beautiful painting."

4. Which sentence uses you're correctly?

5. Which sentence uses its correctly?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why is it important to use the correct commonly confused words in writing? Explain your answer and give two examples.
