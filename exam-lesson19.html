📝 Quiz 19: Sentence Combining and Paragraph Development

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 19.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the term being described.

1. The process of joining two or more related sentences to form a clear sentence.

2. Words used to connect words, phrases, or clauses.

3. The group of seven coordinating conjunctions remembered through the word FANBOYS.

4. A conjunction that connects a dependent clause to an independent clause.

5. A word such as "who," "which," or "that" used to introduce a clause about a noun.

6. The process of expanding a main idea using supporting information.

7. Information that provides specific situations or items to explain an idea.

8. Information that can be checked or proven to support a topic.

9. The unnecessary use of the same words or ideas repeatedly in writing.

10. A word or phrase that helps connect ideas and improve the flow of writing.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Choose the letter of the best answer.

1. What is sentence combining?

2. Which of the following is a coordinating conjunction?

3. Which sentence correctly combines the ideas "It was raining" and "We stayed inside"?

4. Which word is a relative pronoun?

5. What is the purpose of paragraph development?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Explain how sentence combining improves writing. Describe how supporting details and examples help develop a paragraph.
