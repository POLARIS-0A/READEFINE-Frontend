🎯 Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • Define context clues.
  • Identify different types of context clues.
  • Determine the possible meaning of unfamiliar words.
  • Use surrounding information to understand a sentence.
💡 Introduction & Discussion

Readers sometimes encounter unfamiliar words while reading. However, they do not always need a dictionary to understand them.

The words and sentences surrounding an unfamiliar word may provide clues to its meaning. These clues are called context clues.

🔍 1. What Are Context Clues?

Context clues are hints or pieces of information found in the surrounding words, phrases, or sentences that help readers understand an unfamiliar word.

✨ Example:
"After walking for several hours under the hot sun, Mark felt exhausted and needed to rest." The phrase "needed to rest" suggests that exhausted means very tired.

📚 2. Types of Context Clues

A. Definition or Explanation Clues

The meaning of an unfamiliar word is directly explained in the sentence.

Example: A habitat, the natural home of an animal, provides food and shelter.
B. Synonym Clues

A familiar word with a similar meaning helps explain the unfamiliar word.

Example: The room was silent and quiet during the examination.
C. Antonym or Contrast Clues

An opposite word or contrasting idea provides a hint.

Example: Unlike his outgoing sister, Daniel is shy and avoids large gatherings.
D. Example Clues

Examples in a sentence help explain an unfamiliar word.

Example: Citrus fruits, such as oranges, lemons, and calamansi, contain vitamin C.
E. Inference Clues

Readers use details in the sentence and their understanding of the situation to determine meaning.

Example: Mia grabbed her umbrella and wore her raincoat before leaving the house.

🪜 3. Steps in Using Context Clues

  1. Read the entire sentence.
  2. Identify the unfamiliar word.
  3. Examine the words around it.
  4. Look for definitions, examples, similarities, or contrasts.
  5. Determine the possible meaning.
  6. Read the sentence again to check whether the meaning makes sense.

💡 Additional Sentence Examples

"The athlete was determined to finish the race despite being tired." Determined means strongly decided or committed to achieving something.
"The instructions were complicated, so the students asked for an explanation." Complicated means difficult to understand or deal with.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • Context clues help readers understand unfamiliar words.
  • Clues may appear in the same sentence or nearby sentences.
  • Common types include definition, synonym, antonym, example, and inference clues.
  • Always consider the entire sentence before deciding on a word's meaning.
