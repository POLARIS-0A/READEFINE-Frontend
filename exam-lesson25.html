📝 Quiz 25: Drawing Conclusions

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 25.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. A judgment or understanding reached after examining information.

2. The process of using details and reasoning to form a logical understanding.

3. Facts, examples, or observations that support a judgment.

4. The process of using logic to connect information.

5. An idea or judgment formed without enough supporting information.

6. Information that helps readers understand a situation.

7. A final judgment supported by available details.

8. The ability to examine information and form reasonable judgments.

9. Details that help explain why a conclusion is reasonable.

10. A statement that presents the result of examining evidence.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. What is the best basis for drawing a conclusion?

2. A student practices spelling every day and makes fewer spelling mistakes. What is the most reasonable conclusion?

3. What makes a conclusion different from a guess?

4. Which of the following is evidence?

5. Why is drawing conclusions important?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Explain how you draw conclusions when reading a story or article. Why should your conclusion be supported by evidence?
