📝 Quiz 34: Writing Coherent and Unified Paragraphs

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 34.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. The quality of a paragraph in which all sentences support one central idea.

2. The quality of a paragraph in which ideas are connected logically and smoothly.

3. A sentence that introduces the main idea of a paragraph.

4. Sentences that explain or provide details about the main idea.

5. A sentence that provides closure to a paragraph.

6. Words or phrases used to connect ideas.

7. A transition word commonly used to introduce an additional idea.

8. A transition word commonly used to show contrast.

9. The central idea developed throughout a paragraph.

10. The process of arranging ideas in a clear and logical order.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. What does paragraph unity mean?

2. What does coherence refer to?

3. Which sentence is most likely a topic sentence?

4. Which transition word shows contrast?

5. Which sentence is unrelated to a paragraph about healthy eating?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Write a short paragraph about the importance of time management. Include a topic sentence, at least two supporting sentences, and a concluding sentence.
