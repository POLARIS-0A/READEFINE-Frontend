📝 Lesson 5 Official Assessment

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 5: Parts of Speech.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the part of speech described.

1. A word that names a person, place, thing, or idea.

2. A word that expresses an action or state of being.

3. A word that describes a noun or pronoun.

4. A word that describes a verb, adjective, or another adverb.

5. A word that takes the place of a noun.

6. A word that shows the relationship of a noun or pronoun to another word.

7. A word that connects words, phrases, or clauses.

8. A word that expresses sudden emotion or feeling.

9. The part of speech of the word quickly.

10. The part of speech of the word beautiful.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Choose the best answer.

1. Which word is a noun?

2. Which word is a verb in the sentence "The students study every night"?

3. Which word is an adjective?

4. Which word is a pronoun?

5. What is the function of a conjunction?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Choose five parts of speech and explain their functions. Give one example of each in a sentence.
