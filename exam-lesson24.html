📝 Quiz 24: Understanding Tone and Author's Purpose

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 24.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. The writer's attitude toward the subject or audience.

2. The main reason a writer creates a text.

3. A tone that uses professional and respectful language.

4. A tone that uses casual and conversational expressions.

5. A tone that creates amusement or laughter.

6. A tone that expresses hope and confidence.

7. A writing purpose that provides facts and knowledge.

8. A writing purpose that attempts to influence the reader.

9. A writing purpose that aims to provide enjoyment.

10. The words and expressions selected by a writer to communicate a message.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. What is the tone of this sentence?
"The results of the investigation must be examined carefully."

2. What is the author's purpose of a newspaper article reporting recent events?

3. Which statement is written to persuade?

4. What should readers examine to identify the tone of a text?

5. What is the difference between tone and author's purpose?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why is it important to understand the tone and author's purpose of a text? Give an example.
