📝 Lesson 1 Official Assessment

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 1.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

1. The collection of words a person knows and uses.

2. A word that has the same or nearly the same meaning as another word.

3. A word that has the opposite meaning of another word.

4. The meaning of a word.

5. The use of words when speaking or writing.

6. A word that describes a person, place, thing, or idea.

7. The process of learning and understanding new words.

8. A word used to name an action.

9. The ability to understand what words mean.

10. A group of words used to communicate an idea.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

1. What does vocabulary refer to?

2. Which word means the same as happy?

3. Which word is the opposite of difficult?

4. Why is vocabulary important?

5. Which activity can help improve vocabulary?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Explain why having a good vocabulary is important in everyday communication. Give at least two examples of how it can help you as a student.
