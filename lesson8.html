Level 1 • Lesson 8

Basic Grammar and Punctuation

← Back to Lessons
🎯 Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • Explain the importance of grammar and punctuation.
  • Identify common punctuation marks.
  • Apply basic capitalization rules.
  • Correct simple grammatical and punctuation errors.
💡 Introduction & Discussion

Grammar provides rules for organizing words into meaningful sentences. Punctuation marks help readers understand how sentences are structured and how ideas are connected.
Without proper grammar and punctuation, written messages may become confusing or difficult to understand.

1. What Is Grammar?

Grammar refers to the rules and principles that guide how words are formed, arranged, and used in a language.

Example:
❌ Incorrect: She go to school every day.
✔️ Correct: She goes to school every day.

2. What Is Punctuation?

Punctuation refers to marks used in writing to separate ideas, indicate pauses, and clarify meaning.

3. Common Punctuation Marks

Period (.) & Comma (,)

• Period: Used at the end of a declarative sentence (Ex: I enjoy reading books.)

• Comma: Used to separate items in a list and indicate pauses (Ex: I bought a notebook, pen, and folder.)

Question Mark (?) & Exclamation (!)

• Question Mark: Used at the end of a direct question (Ex: Where is your classroom?)

• Exclamation Point: Used to express strong feelings (Ex: That is amazing!)

Apostrophe (') & Quotation Marks (" ")

• Apostrophe: Shows possession or contractions (Ex: Maria's notebook, Don't, It's)

• Quotation Marks: Shows direct speech or quotes (Ex: She said, "I am ready.")

Colon (:)

• Colon: Used to introduce a list, explanation, or example (Ex: Bring the following items: paper, pen, and notebook.)

4. Capitalization Rules

Use capital letters:

  • At the beginning of a sentence.
  • For proper nouns such as names of people and places.
  • For days and months.
  • For the pronoun "I."
  • In official titles when required.
Example:
❌ Incorrect: maria lives in batangas.
✔️ Correct: Maria lives in Batangas.

5. Common Grammar Errors

  • Subject-Verb Agreement: He play basketball. ➡️ He plays basketball.
  • Incorrect Verb Form: She have a new laptop. ➡️ She has a new laptop.
  • Sentence Fragment: Because I was busy. ➡️ I could not attend because I was busy.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • Grammar organizes words correctly.
  • Punctuation clarifies written messages.
  • Capitalization follows specific writing rules.
  • Correct grammar makes communication clearer.
