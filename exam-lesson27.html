📝 Quiz 27: Critical Reading and Analysis

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 27.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. An active process of questioning and evaluating written information.

2. The process of examining parts of a text to understand how ideas are developed.

3. A statement or position that a writer wants readers to accept.

4. Facts or examples used to support a claim.

5. An idea accepted as true without sufficient proof.

6. A statement presented by a writer as something readers should believe.

7. The process of examining whether an argument is reasonable and supported.

8. A source of information used to support an argument.

9. A possible weakness in an argument caused by insufficient supporting information.

10. The ability to think carefully and evaluate information before accepting it.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. What should a critical reader do?

2. Which of the following is a claim?

3. Which of the following is evidence?

4. What is an assumption?

5. Why should readers evaluate arguments?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Explain why critical reading is important when using social media, websites, and other sources of information. Give one example of how you can apply it.
