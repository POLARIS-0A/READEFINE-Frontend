📝 Quiz 20: Summarizing and Paraphrasing

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 20.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Direction: Identify the term being described.

1. The process of presenting the most important ideas of a text in a shorter form.

2. The process of restating information using different words and sentence structures while keeping the original meaning.

3. The central message or most important point of a text.

4. A shorter version of a text that includes only its most important information.

5. The use of different words with similar meanings when restating a sentence.

6. Changing the arrangement of words and sentences while preserving the original meaning.

7. Presenting another person's words or ideas as one's own without giving proper credit.

8. A quality of a good summary that means the information must keep the original meaning.

9. A quality of writing that avoids adding personal opinions not found in the original text.

10. The practice of acknowledging the original source of borrowed information.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Direction: Choose the letter of the best answer.

1. What is summarizing?

2. What is paraphrasing?

3. Which statement best describes the difference between summarizing and paraphrasing?

4. Which of the following is an example of plagiarism?

5. What should a writer do when paraphrasing information from a source?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Explain the difference between summarizing and paraphrasing. Why are these two skills important for students when reading and writing academic work?
