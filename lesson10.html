Level 1 • Lesson 10

Reading and Understanding Short Texts

← Back to Lessons
🎯 Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • Explain reading comprehension.
  • Identify the main idea of a short passage.
  • Recognize supporting details.
  • Answer questions based on a text.
  • Make simple conclusions from information presented.
💡 Introduction & Discussion

Reading is not only about recognizing words. It also involves understanding the message, identifying important information, and connecting ideas.
Reading comprehension is an important skill because it helps individuals understand textbooks, instructions, news articles, online information, and everyday written communication.

1. What Is Reading Comprehension?

Reading comprehension is the ability to read, understand, interpret, and gain meaning from written information. A reader with good comprehension can identify important ideas, recognize details, and explain what a text communicates.

2. Main Idea

The main idea is the central point or most important message of a paragraph or passage. It answers the question: "What is the text mainly about?"

Example Passage:
"Reading regularly helps students improve their vocabulary. It also strengthens their understanding of different topics and develops their ability to express ideas."

🎯 Main Idea: Reading regularly helps students improve their learning and communication skills.

3. Supporting Details

Supporting details are facts, examples, explanations, or information that develop and explain the main idea.

Supporting Details from the Example:
• Reading improves vocabulary.
• Reading strengthens understanding of different topics.
• Reading develops communication skills.

4. Making Conclusions

A conclusion is an idea or judgment reached after considering the information presented in a text.

Example Passage:
"Anna reviewed her notes every night, completed practice exercises, and asked questions whenever she did not understand a topic."

🎯 Conclusion: Anna is actively preparing for her examination.

5. Reading Strategies

  • Preview the Text: Look at the title, headings, and other visible information before reading.
  • Identify Important Information: Pay attention to key words, repeated ideas, and important details.
  • Ask Questions: Ask yourself what the passage is about and why certain information is important.
  • Summarize: Restate the main points using your own words.
  • Reread When Necessary: Read difficult parts again to improve understanding.

6. Sample Reading Passage

The Importance of Time Management
Time management is an important skill for students. It helps them organize their activities, complete assignments, and prepare for examinations. Students who manage their time properly can balance academic responsibilities with personal activities. Creating a schedule and setting priorities are two useful ways to manage time effectively.

Comprehension Answers:
• Main Idea: Time management is an important skill for students.
• Supporting Details: Helps complete assignments; helps prepare for exams.
• Ways to Manage Time: Creating a schedule; setting priorities.
• Conclusion: They can organize responsibilities and balance schoolwork with personal activities.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • Reading comprehension means understanding and interpreting written information.
  • The main idea is the central message.
  • Supporting details explain or develop the main idea.
  • Conclusions are based on information presented in a text.
  • Previewing, questioning, summarizing, and rereading improve comprehension.
