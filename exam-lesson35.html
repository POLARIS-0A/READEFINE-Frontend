📝 Quiz 35: Essay Writing Fundamentals

Complete all test items below based on Lesson 35.

Part I. Identification (10 points)

Directions: Identify the term being described.

1. A structured piece of writing that discusses a particular subject.

2. The opening part of an essay that introduces the topic.

3. The central statement that presents the main idea of an essay.

4. The main section that develops and explains the essay's ideas.

5. The final part that summarizes the main points.

6. A type of essay that tells a story or personal experience.

7. A type of essay that describes a person, place, object, or experience.

8. A type of essay that explains a topic using facts and information.

9. A type of essay that presents a position supported by reasons and evidence.

10. The process of improving the content and organization of a written draft.

Part II. Multiple Choice (5 points)

Directions: Choose the letter of the correct answer.

1. Which part of an essay introduces the topic?

2. What is the main purpose of a thesis statement?

3. Which type of essay tells a story?

4. What is the purpose of revising?

5. Which is the correct general structure of an essay?

Part III. Essay (5 points)

Why is essay writing an important skill for students? Explain how the introduction, body, and conclusion contribute to an effective essay.
