Level 1 • Lesson 6

Basic Sentence Structure

← Back to Lessons
🎯 Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • 1. Define a sentence.
  • 2. Identify the basic parts of a sentence.
  • 3. Distinguish complete sentences from fragments.
  • 4. Construct clear and meaningful sentences.
💡 Introduction & Discussion

A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought. Sentences allow people to share information, ask questions, give instructions, and express feelings.
A well-constructed sentence helps readers understand the intended message.

📌 1. What Is a Sentence?

A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought and follows grammatical rules.

Example: The students completed their project.
This is a complete sentence because it communicates a complete idea.

🧩 2. Basic Sentence Elements

Subject

The subject tells who or what the sentence is about.

Example: The teacher explained the lesson.

Predicate

The predicate tells something about the subject. It usually contains a verb.

Example: The teacher explained the lesson.

📐 3. Basic Sentence Patterns

  • Subject + Verb (S–V): Birds fly.
  • Subject + Verb + Object (S–V–O): Maria reads books.
  • Subject + Linking Verb + Complement: John is intelligent. (The complement describes or identifies the subject.)

⚠️ 4. Complete Sentences and Sentence Fragments

Complete Sentence

Contains a complete thought.
Ex: The students attended the seminar.

Sentence Fragment

An incomplete group of words.
Ex: Because the students were late. (Leaves reader wondering what happened).

📋 5. Basic Rules of Sentence Construction

  • Begin a sentence with a capital letter.
  • Use correct word order.
  • Include a subject and a predicate in a basic complete sentence.
  • Use appropriate punctuation.
  • Make sure the sentence expresses a complete thought.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • A sentence expresses a complete thought.
  • The subject tells who or what the sentence is about, while the predicate tells something about it.
  • Sentence fragments are incomplete thoughts that need revision.
  • Correct sentence construction improves clarity and communication.
